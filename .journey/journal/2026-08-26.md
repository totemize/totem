## Atomic resource brokerage

Executed Phase HW-01 Plan 07 entirely on the local controller. The canonical,
provider-free resource broker now normalizes claims across GPIO, SPI, I2C,
block, path, PHY, interface, adapter, D-Bus, socket, credential, and
compatibility resources. It validates every claim in a complete binding-plan

## Explicit lifecycle and process supervision

Executed Phase HW-01 Plan 09 on the local controller only. The canonical
runtime now has a locked explicit binding lifecycle, immutable health
snapshots, monotonic bounded lifecycle events, required-versus-optional
readiness, and idempotent host-first close semantics. The default isolated
host now groups bindings by provider identity/version (with a per-binding
override), validates session/generation/binding membership in `host.ready`,

Recovery follows the safety order from the process/lease spike: invalidate
correlations, terminate, escalate to kill, positively wait for exit, close the
carrier, then release the exact generation lease. Recovery uses bounded

controlled restart. Source review also moved worker binding validation before
provider dispatch and made aggregate/event snapshot projections fully
immutable. These safeguards stay local controller evidence; Pi timing,
resource handoff, and sandbox-strength evidence remain deferred.
